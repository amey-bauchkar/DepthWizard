Single-view height estimation & 3D surface reconstruction

Turn one aerial photograph or GeoTIFF into a surface model. Mode A gives a relative surface; Mode B anchors it to a DEM to produce a calibrated, georeferenced DSM with traceable quality.

BCalibrated DSM
GeoTIFF + DEM. Metres, EGM2008 vertical reference unless stated.

          DepthWizard
          
Vertical reference of absolute outputs: EGM2008 unless stated. Measurements are always sampled server-side from DSM rasters — never from the rendered mesh. The 3D viewer is a visual representation only.

        

        

          
Calibration tiers

          

            
R
Relative — no units, no scale.

            
T
Absolute DSM = DEM + tiled model detail scaled against the DEM (unvalidated unless anchors / reference).

            
A
Anchor-refined — terrain datum + detail gain from ground control.

            
H
Metric heights above ground from the fine-tuned nDSM model (when installed; no absolute elevation without a DEM).
